Privacy notice

How to request deletion

IntFitout is operated by AL-BABA Interiors. To request a review of information relating to you for deletion, email Osaid Jamal Baba at office.albaba@gmail.com.

Suggested subject: IntFitout data deletion request

In your initial email, provide:

  • Your name.
  • The phone number associated with the information, if relevant.
  • A description of the information you want reviewed for deletion.

Do not include passwords, payment details or identity documents in the initial email.

What happens next

Deletion requests are initially reviewed and handled manually by Osaid. He may ask for enough information to verify the request's connection to you. He will communicate what can be removed and any records that need to remain.

What a request means

Sending an email does not automatically erase records. Removal depends on the manual review of the information involved and any work, payroll or financial records that need to remain.

Deleting a worker record does not by itself ensure removal of related assignments, payroll snapshots, uploads, logs, backups or copies held by service providers. These records and copies must be considered separately; this page does not promise complete deletion.
